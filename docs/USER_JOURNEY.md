# User Journey

## Before Proyecto SER

Before using Proyecto SER, life often feels fragmented.

The user may be carrying mental noise, a lack of direction, and a sense that effort is not leading to clarity. Days can feel reactive and inconsistent. Emotions may be crowded, and attention may be scattered.

There is often a quiet desire for steadiness, but not always a clear path to reach it.

---

## First Day

The user opens the app with a simple need: to slow down.

They are welcomed calmly.

There is no pressure, no overload, and no demand to perform. The experience feels gentle from the beginning.

Their only task is to complete Today.

That first step is small, but meaningful. It offers a sense of progress without demanding perfection.

---

## First Week

In the first week, trust begins to form.

Daily rituals become familiar. The user starts to recognize that consistency does not require intensity. Writing begins to feel easier, and reflection becomes less foreign.

What once felt like an interruption starts to feel like a return.

---

## First Month

After a month, the app begins to feel like part of everyday life.

The user starts noticing patterns in their moods, their habits, and their energy. They begin to respond with more intention and less reaction.

The experience is not about fixing everything at once. It is about becoming more aware of what matters.

---

## First Year

Over time, Proyecto SER becomes a place where the user organizes their inner life.

Not because they have to.

Because they want to.

It becomes a steady companion in moments of reflection, uncertainty, growth, and change.

---

## Product Promise

Proyecto SER does not promise success.

It promises clarity.

It does not promise discipline.

It helps build it.

It does not promise happiness.

It helps the user understand themselves.
